The site is served as static files by GitHub Pages. GitHub may keep standard server logs (IP address, requested page, time) as part of operating the service. We do not have access to those logs and do not combine them with anything else.

5. What we do not do

6. Your choices

You can clear local storage from your browser settings at any time; the site will simply ask again for language and display choices. You can block advertising cookies with a browser setting or extension without losing any content on this site.
