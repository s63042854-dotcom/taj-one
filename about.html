About TAJ ONE

TAJ ONE for General Trading and Energy is a private company based in Erbil, Iraq, working in petroleum derivative supply, transport and procurement services for institutional buyers.

What the company does

  • Supply and delivery of diesel, gasoline and kerosene to commercial and institutional buyers in the Kurdistan Region and federal Iraq.
  • Transport and logistics coordination for bulk fuel movements, including documentation, escort requirements and delivery scheduling.
  • Procurement advisory for organisations preparing tenders: reading scope, matching categories, and building compliant price schedules.

Registration

Registered supplier on the United Nations Global Marketplace under number #1277583. The company also maintains supplier registrations with several UN agencies and follows their procurement portals directly.

What this website is

This site is a free reference tool. It publishes Iraqi official retail fuel schedules, the central bank and market exchange rates, international reference prices, and a margin calculator for traders. It exists because the same numbers are scattered across paper schedules, social media posts and unverified channels, and traders need one place where the arithmetic is transparent.

Every figure on the site carries the date it was taken. When a number has not been refreshed, we show the older date rather than an estimate. We never present an old price as today's price.

Editorial rules

  • Fuel prices are official retail schedules as published, not offers for sale.
  • Exchange rates are shown as reported, with the difference between official and market rates kept visible rather than averaged.
  • We publish no forecasts and no trading signals. The site explains how a margin is computed; it does not tell anyone what to buy.
  • Corrections are made in place, and the date line is updated whenever a figure changes.
